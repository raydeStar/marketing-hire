# HireZero marketing video: your recording script

**Main cut: 84 seconds. Eight short clips. No generated audio.**

Speak to one founder who is tired of holding every marketing task in their head.
Warm, direct, and conversational; no announcer voice. Let “you keep the final
say” feel reassuring. Pronounce HireZero as “Hire Zero,” and Claw like the word.

Record one clip per scene, using the numbers below as filenames. Leave about
one second of quiet before and after each take. WAV is ideal; your phone's normal
audio format is also fine. The times are editing targets, not a speed test: we
can retime the pictures to your natural delivery. Do not read the headings or
the visual directions aloud.

## 01 — Meet HireZero · 00:00–00:08

**Picture:** HireZero mark, “Marketing lead,” and the cockpit.

> You built the business. Marketing still needs attention. Meet HireZero: a marketing employee with a desk of its own.

## 02 — Teach the business · 00:08–00:19

**Picture:** The business brief: audience, voice, goals, and boundaries.

> Start with your business: what you sell, who you serve, and what you can honestly claim. Claw keeps that context beside the work.

## 03 — Assign the work · 00:19–00:29

**Picture:** A concrete assignment and the employee's prepared response.

> Give it an outcome. For this introduction, the brief is simple: show useful work, speak to founders, and avoid unsupported claims.

## 04 — Make the decision · 00:29–00:41

**Picture:** The lead recommendation and its attached work.

> The cockpit brings the decision into focus: why this matters, what is prepared, and what Claw recommends. Review the package, change direction, or park it.

## 05 — Review the package · 00:41–00:53

**Picture:** The campaign's pieces, review grades, claims, and blockers.

> Open the campaign and the pieces stay together: the plan, the central angle, drafts, page copy, and the details still waiting on you.

## 06 — Guide the revision · 00:53–01:06

**Picture:** The actual comparison component, showing an illustrative before and after.

> Feedback has somewhere to go. Compare the earlier draft with the revision, check the supporting claims, and send back a precise note when something needs work.

## 07 — Stay in control · 01:06–01:16

**Picture:** Responsive browser views of the cockpit and campaign.

> Your judgment stays in the loop. Review from your desk or your phone, and choose the action before anything ships.

## 08 — Close · 01:16–01:24

**Picture:** HireZero mark and hirezero.app.

> HireZero. Prepared work. A clear decision. Your marketing lead, with you in charge.

## Before the competition cut

This is the marketing-preview script. The present pictures use the real interface
with fictional example content, labelled throughout. They do not demonstrate
live model execution, SMS delivery, customer results, or OpenClaw multiplayer.

After the separate release task has verified a real run, replace the example
assignment and campaign scenes with the real, approved result. Add the native
multiplayer handoff and phone continuity footage when available. Any new voice
lines should describe only what those recordings actually show. Keep the final
competition cut at least 60 seconds long.
