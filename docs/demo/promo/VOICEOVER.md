# HireZero promo: optional voiceover

The promo works with music alone: every line of copy is on screen. If you
narrate, read these lines against the picture. Each line fits inside its scene
at a relaxed pace, so you have room to breathe. Keep your voice confident and
warm, like you're telling a friend about a great hire. Don't use an announcer
voice. Say "Hire Zero".

Record one take per line (or one continuous take while watching the video),
leaving a beat of silence around each. Send the files over and the mix will duck
the music under your voice.

| # | Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:08 | Cold open | "You built the product. Now people need to find it. And marketing is still on your list." |
| 2 | 0:08–0:12 | HireZero logo | "Meet HireZero, your AI marketing lead." |
| 3 | 0:12–0:20 | Business brief | "Brief it once: what you sell, who it's for, your voice, and only the claims you can back up." |
| 4 | 0:20–0:28 | Start shift | "Then it works the shift. It reads your site, sizes up the competition, and drafts the week." |
| 5 | 0:28–0:38 | First shift results, review desk | "You wake up to finished work. Every piece is graded, every claim is shown." |
| 6 | 0:38–0:44 | Revision and lesson | "Give it a note, and it rewrites and remembers the lesson." |
| 7 | 0:44–0:52 | Approve all 7 posts | "A week of posts, approved in one tap. Nothing goes out without you." |
| 8 | 0:52–1:00 | Phones, then team | "Review from anywhere. Bring your team. You keep the final say." |
| 9 | 1:00–1:08 | "So you can get back to building.", then the logo | "So you can get back to building. HireZero: your marketing lead, with you in charge." |

The music drops at 0:08 and 0:44. Let those two moments land: start line 2 just
after the hit, and say "one tap" right as the posts turn green.

## What the picture is

- Real HireZero UI captures, with sample workspace content: the business brief,
  the campaign review desk, phone views, and team roles and permissions.
- Motion-graphic recreations styled after real product surfaces and their
  labels: the shift card and its eight phases, the "Watching it work" feed, the
  first-shift results, the revision diff, the lesson, and "Approve all N posts".
  The feed lines, post titles, grades and site fixes are illustrative sample
  content, not a recorded run.
- Approving records the owner's decision. HireZero does not auto-post; each post
  then offers its network's composer or a reminder. The copy says exactly this.
