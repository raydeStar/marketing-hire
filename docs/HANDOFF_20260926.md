## What the employee can do now

| New | What you'll see | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Videos** | A task asking for a video or clip gets a storyboard of 4–8 scenes, which the host renders as a branded MP4 with ffmpeg: vertical, square or landscape, a sound-off hook first and an accent call-to-action last. A scene can show a **screenshot of a page on your own site**, framed beside or below the words. The caption becomes a draft post. **Record narration** then **Render video** adds your own voice | Library → Campaigns → Videos |
| **Post images** | A social draft can come with a branded image card (a number, short claim or quote) at the network's size | Library → Campaigns → Images |
| **Series** | "Three LinkedIn posts" or "an onboarding email sequence" becomes separate drafts. Social posts are plain text (no Markdown). Hacker News, Reddit and Product Hunt drafts lead with `Title:`. A part with nowhere to post, such as a Product Hunt listing, is kept as draft text | Needs your decision |
| **Long-form** | Blog posts and plans over about 600 words are written in parts, so nothing is cut short. Drafts can run to 12,000 characters (the ledger cut them at 4,000) | Blog drafts → your CMS |
| **Proposed experiments** | Hypothesis, the change, metric, length, threshold and ICE score, saved as **Proposed**. Nothing runs until you press **Start**; **Decline** asks why | Work → Scorecard |
| **Where visits come from** | With Google Analytics connected: sessions and key events by channel and landing page, with conversion, in shift plans and the weekly and monthly reports | Automatic after connecting |
| **Search opportunities** | With Search Console connected, queries ranked 4–20 with real impressions become a signal, and page-copy and blog work aims at them | Automatic after connecting |
| **Customer notes** | Call notes you file there are quoted, with citations, in interview syntheses, positioning, objections and case studies | Library → Research → Customer notes |
| **Suggested posting times** | "Tue 8:30 AM (suggested)" on approved posts. It starts from each network's habits; after five posts with results it uses your own best times | Chat updates on approved drafts |
| **Decision log** | Every approval, rejection, rating, page-copy decision and experiment decision, with your reason | Library → Company → Decision log |
| **Monthly report** | In the first week of each month: last month against the month before, from the records, at no model cost | Work → This week; Reports/Monthly |

Also:

- Public copy speaks to the reader as "you", and launch QA flags "the owner" and "the user".
- Documents list their sources once.

## What the live runs found (and what was fixed)

Three shifts on the test workspace ran against a local copy of the site's CMS, using 180,434 tokens in 47 turns, metered and receipted in the ledger. They produced:
- a launch-week plan;
- a Show HN draft;
- LinkedIn posts;
- a 3-email onboarding sequence;
- a launch email;
- two blog posts (one sent through the site connector into the CMS as a draft, checked in the admin);
- an interview kit;
- two battlecards, the second with real prices (Jasper $69 or $59 a seat, Lindy $29.99, $99.99 and $199.99);
- two rendered videos (a 28-second vertical and a 45-second landscape with a site screenshot);
- a Product Hunt kit;
- a LinkedIn post with its own image card;
- a hackathon announcement split into X and Bluesky drafts, with Mastodon kept as draft text because no server is connected.

| Problem found live | Fix |
|---|---|
| The self-review judged drafts **cut short by the packet trimmer**, then "fixed" the truncation | The work under review is trimmed last |
| A turn marked "unknown" **froze the shift**, although its usage had been reported | `shift-heal` settles it from the report; the host heals before its next claim |
| **Test runs reached the real employee container** and marked a live turn unknown | Test hosts can't reach real containers |
| "Run a cycle now" was **cancelled when the page or client disconnected** mid-turn | Cycles run until done; only a host shutdown stops them |
| A **blog post was lost** because its rationale plus review note exceeded the ledger's 1,000 characters | Rationales are fitted; a draft that can't be saved is kept as draft text |
| A video was rejected because the storyboard came as a **JSON object** | Both forms accepted |
| **Three posts arrived as one draft**, with Markdown that LinkedIn would show literally | Series, and per-network formatting |
| **Show HN draft had no title** | `Title:` line |
| **Prices missing** from the battlecard although the pages were read | Prices lead each page excerpt |
| Emails said "**the owner** approves your access" to customers | Voice rule plus a QA check |
| A small token limit **starved shifts** (each turn holds 25,000 tokens) | Explained in the shift panel; the default limit (10M) is unaffected |
| An **assigned task was skipped** on the employee's own judgement | Free plan slots take the next assigned tasks |
| A series came back as **one body of "Channel: …" parts**, then each post **began with its channel's name** | The host splits the body into drafts and takes off labels the review adds |
| A placeholder "[add the approved Product Hunt URL…]" **passed launch QA** | QA catches `[add …]`, `[your …]` and `[url]` |
| Tables the employee writes showed as **raw pipes** in the Library | Rows read as labelled lists |

## Still weak

- **Copy quality is competent, not memorable.** The self-review often names the gap (generic hook, one clear next step). Your verdicts and reasons are what sharpen it.
- **The model sometimes ignores the requested format.** The host now repairs the patterns seen tonight (a series in one body, a storyboard as an object, labels). Expect new ones; each is a small host-side fix.
- **Tables are flattened to lists.** Real table rendering needs the `remark-gfm` package plus a third-party notice update; worth doing when packaging.
- **Posting still needs your hand or your developer apps.** Videos and images are attached when you post: no network connector uploads media yet.
- **ffmpeg must be on the PATH for videos and images; it isn't bundled.** For Plow, the image needs `ffmpeg`, `fonts-dejavu` and Chromium (for screenshots).
- **Hackathon gates are not done:** native multiplayer check, public MIT repo, Agent Index client and a demo video of 60 seconds or more. See [HACKATHON_SUBMISSION.md](HACKATHON_SUBMISSION.md).
